Who We Are

Behera STEM School is a fully governmental institution established in 2025 in Damanhour, Behera Governorate, serving high-achieving students in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics under the Egyptian Ministry of Education.

Foundation

Our Identity

  • Governmental STEM school
  • Established 2025
  • Supervised by Central Unit for STEM Schools and Gifted Education Affairs
  • Community focused on preparing future scientists, engineers, and innovators
Learning Methods

Our Approach

Independent Learning

Students develop critical thinking and self-directed inquiry skills through rigorous academic standards and individual research initiatives.

Project-Based Learning

Practical, hands-on projects that integrate multiple disciplines, requiring students to solve real-world problems collaboratively.

Capstone Research

Comprehensive scientific research projects that culminate student learning, emphasizing original investigation and academic rigor.
