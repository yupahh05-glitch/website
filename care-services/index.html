Care Services

Supporting a safe, organized, and structured residential environment for all students at STEM School – Behera.

Department Overview

The Care Services Department at Behera STEM School is responsible for maintaining a safe, clean, and well-organized residential environment. The department supervises boarding facilities and ensures that students live in conditions that support their academic focus and daily well-being.

Responsibilities

  • Residential supervision
  • Cleanliness and hygiene
  • Meal quality and nutrition
  • Facility maintenance
  • Safety and security

Operations & Services

Residential Management

Supervision of dormitories, room organization, and daily residential operations.

Nutrition & Dining

Provision of healthy meals and monitoring of food quality and hygiene standards.

Maintenance & Safety

Continuous maintenance of facilities and implementation of safety procedures.

Leadership

Major General Magdy Mohie

Administrative Manager of Care Services

He oversees daily operations related to accommodation, safety, nutrition, and facility management, ensuring a secure and supportive residential environment.

Colonel Hossam Sharaf El-Din

Co-Administrative Manager of Care Services

His role includes supervision and coordination of administrative functions to ensure quality standards across student services.

A Structured Environment for Academic Success

The Care Services system at Behera STEM School is designed to create a structured and supportive living environment that allows students to focus fully on their academic responsibilities while living safely within a well-managed residential community.
