ADMISSIONS

STEM Schools for Science and Technology

Admission to STEM Schools for Science and Technology is a highly selective, merit-based process designed to identify students who demonstrate exceptional academic achievement, advanced analytical thinking, and strong potential in scientific and mathematical reasoning. The admission framework is established annually by the relevant educational authorities, and criteria may be subject to periodic revision.

01

Academic Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy one of the officially approved academic conditions. A student may qualify by obtaining 98% (274.5 out of 280) in the Preparatory Certificate without achieving full marks in any of the three core subjects: Mathematics, Science, and English.

Alternatively, a student may qualify by obtaining 95% (266 out of 280) provided that they have achieved full marks in at least one of the following subjects: Mathematics, Science, or English.

These score thresholds are determined each academic year and may vary in accordance with national admission regulations.
02

Nationality Requirement

Admission is restricted to Egyptian nationals. All applicants must provide official documentation verifying nationality as part of the application process.
03

Educational Background

Students holding the General Preparatory Certificate must meet the previously stated academic requirements.

Students graduating from Al-Azhar preparatory institutes are required to achieve the equivalent qualifying total, calculated based on performance in five principal subjects: Science, Mathematics, English, Arabic, and Social Studies.

Applicants who have completed their preparatory education abroad must undergo an official academic equivalency process to ensure compliance with national standards.
04

Tuition Structure

STEM Schools operate under a partially subsidized financial model. Tuition obligations vary according to the applicant's previous school classification.

Students transferring from government Arabic or experimental language schools are required to pay an annual tuition fee of 5,000 EGP, representing a portion of the total educational cost, with the remaining amount funded by the state.

Students previously enrolled in private schools are required to pay tuition equivalent to the fees of their former institution.

Students transferring from national or international schools are required to pay 30,000 EGP annually.

All fees are subject to official regulation and may be adjusted when necessary.
05

Application and School Preferences

Eligible students must submit the required documents, including the official electronic certificate, through the designated application platform during the announced submission period.

Applicants are required to rank their preferred schools from a total of 23 STEM schools nationwide. It is important to note that certain schools are designated exclusively for boys, others exclusively for girls, while some operate as co-educational institutions. Applicants are responsible for reviewing each school's designation before confirming their preference order.
06

Admission Examination

All eligible applicants must sit for the official STEM Admission Examination. The assessment evaluates proficiency in Science, Mathematics, and English, in addition to measuring intelligence, logical reasoning, and analytical aptitude.

Candidates must achieve a minimum score above the 50% threshold in order to qualify for further consideration.
07

Selection and Placement

Following the examination, applicants are ranked nationally according to their total admission test score. Final acceptance is determined based on ranking, institutional capacity, and the applicant's ordered school preferences.

In 2025, the total number of admitted students ranged between 2,000 and 2,500 nationwide, although this figure remains subject to adjustment based on annual policy decisions.

Placement in a specific school depends on the candidate's score, national ranking, and the availability of seats within their selected preferences.
08

Final Enrollment Procedures

Students who receive official acceptance must complete the enrollment process by submitting all required original documents within the designated timeframe. Failure to finalize documentation may result in cancellation of admission.
