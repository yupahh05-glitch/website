Competitions | Scientific Events

STEM School – Behera students demonstrate academic excellence and research innovation through participation in regional and international competitions. Our students engage in:

  • Science Fairs and Research Exhibitions
  • Robotics and Engineering Competitions
  • Innovation and Product Design Contests
  • STEM Olympiads and Challenges
  • Academic Research Conferences

These competitions provide opportunities to showcase scholarly work, receive expert feedback, and gain recognition for intellectual achievement.

Community Impact

Student responsibility extends beyond the classroom. Our students actively engage with their community through organized initiatives:

  • Environmental and Conservation Campaigns
  • Public Health and Awareness Programs
  • Educational Outreach to Younger Students
  • Local Volunteer and Service Projects
  • Civic Leadership and Advocacy Initiatives

These experiences cultivate social responsibility, deepen understanding of real-world challenges, and prepare students to contribute meaningfully to society.
