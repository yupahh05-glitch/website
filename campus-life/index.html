Campus | Infrastructure

An integrated academic and residential environment designed to support advanced STEM learning

Campus Overview

The campus of STEM School – Behera is designed as an integrated environment that includes academic facilities, residential buildings, and outdoor spaces. The layout supports both academic activities and daily student life within a structured and secure setting.

Key Areas:

  • Academic Building: Collaborative learning spaces and classrooms
  • Student Residence: Supervised accommodation and daily facilities
  • Outdoor Spaces: Recreation and social gathering areas

Academic Building

The academic building is designed to support collaborative and active learning through purposeful classroom design and rotational systems.

  • 15 Classrooms: Equipped for interactive instruction and group work
  • Rotational System: Students move between specialist learning spaces to maintain engagement and fresh perspectives
  • Collaborative Groups: Each classroom organized for peer discussion and project-based learning
  • Flexible Learning Spaces: Designed for discussion, teamwork, and active research-based instruction

Laboratories | Technical Facilities

The school provides specialized laboratories and technical facilities that allow students to apply scientific concepts through practical experimentation and hands-on learning.

  • Physics Laboratory: Mechanics, electricity, optics, and modern physics experiments
  • Chemistry Laboratory: Organic, inorganic, and analytical chemistry practical work
  • Biology Laboratory: Physiology, microbiology, and life sciences investigation
  • Computer | Programming Lab: Software development and computational problem-solving
  • Robotics | Electronics Lab: Embedded systems, automation, and circuit design
  • Engineering Workshop (FabLab): Prototyping, 3D design, machining, and fabrication
  • Earth Science | Geology Lab: Mineralogy, petrology, and geological analysis
  • Environmental Science Lab: Ecology, sustainability, and field research methods
  • Research Resource Center: Library, digital resources, and research support

Outdoor | Social Spaces

Outdoor spaces are designed to support student interaction, collaboration, and physical activity within a safe and supervised environment.

Central Gathering Area (Pergola)

A shaded pavilion structure used for group discussions, informal learning, and social gatherings. Provides weather protection and encourages outdoor collaboration.

Volleyball Court

Dedicated sports facility supporting physical education and recreational activities.

Football Field

Large outdoor field for team sports, athletic training, and physical development programs.

Note: An indoor gymnasium facility is not currently available. Physical education and sports activities utilize outdoor facilities.

Residential Facilities

STEM School – Behera operates a full residential system to provide a supportive and structured environment that enables students to focus on academics while developing social responsibility and community engagement.

  • Supervised Accommodation: 24/7 residential staff supervision and duty systems ensure safety and student welfare
  • Living Standards: Clean, well-maintained rooms and common areas with regular maintenance and hygiene protocols
  • Nutritional Meals: Daily meals prepared with nutritional oversight and diverse menu planning
  • Security | Safety: Comprehensive safety monitoring, access control, and emergency response systems

School Community

Our school community is built on collaboration, innovation, and shared purpose. Students, faculty, and staff work together to create an environment that fosters academic excellence and personal growth.

Community Highlights:

  • Collaborative research partnerships
  • Student-led innovation initiatives
  • Cross-disciplinary academic discussions
  • Mentorship and peer learning programs
  • Community service and outreach activities
