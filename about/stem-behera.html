STEM Behera

STEM Behera represents the school's mission and curriculum structure: an integrated STEM curriculum, project-based learning, and a capstone research requirement. The program aligns with national standards and aims to prepare students for tertiary education and research careers, emphasizing inquiry, laboratory skills, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
