The STEM Learning Model

Our curriculum is built on five foundational pillars that shape how students learn and think.

  • Inquiry-Based Learning: Students ask questions | investigate problems | and discover answers through systematic exploration.
  • Project-Based Learning (PBL): Real-world challenges drive collaborative problem-solving over extended periods.
  • Interdisciplinary Integration: Subjects connect through common themes | and problems | building holistic understanding.
  • Research | Experimentation: Hands-on laboratory work and data collection are central to every STEM course.
  • Critical Thinking | Problem Solving: Students develop analytical skills to tackle complex | open-ended challenges.

Learning focuses on application and innovation rather than memorization. Students develop the ability to learn independently | adapt to new information | and create solutions that matter.

Capstone Research Project

The Capstone Research Project is the cornerstone of our academic program | where students apply all their learning to solve authentic problems.

  • Multi-Semester Teams: Students work in groups over 6–12 months on original research or engineering design.
  • Real-World Challenges: Projects address local | national | or global problems with measurable outcomes.
  • Original Research: Data collection | experiments | literature review | and hypothesis testing.
  • Prototype Development: Building functional models | systems | or solutions.
  • Rigorous Analysis: Statistical evaluation | error analysis | and peer review.
  • Professional Presentation: Written reports | oral defense | and potential external dissemination.

Every student completes a rigorous capstone project demonstrating mastery of scientific research | technical communication | and collaborative problem-solving.

Assessment Philosophy: We emphasize continuous feedback and growth. Grades reflect multiple dimensions of learning—not just test performance. Students are encouraged to take intellectual risks and learn from failure.

Grading System

Grade Percentage Range Academic Standing
A+ 95–100% Exceptional mastery | innovation
A 90–94% Advanced understanding
B+ 85–89% Strong grasp of concepts
B 80–84% Solid foundational knowledge
C+ 75–79% Satisfactory understanding
C 70–74% Basic competency
D+ 65–69% Needs improvement
D 60–64% Minimal passing
F Below 60% Does not meet requirements

Context: Most courses are offered at the honors level with rigorous standards. A–B grades are typical for students demonstrating mastery. Students may retake assessments to improve grades | reflecting a growth mindset approach.
